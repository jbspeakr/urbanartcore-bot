TASK: Write one Bluesky post that makes people want to open a story from the Urban Art Core archive.

THE STORY:
{POST_DETAILS}

It was first published in {ORIGINAL_MONTH_YEAR}. Its link is {POST_URI}

WHAT TO WRITE:
1. Talk about the story itself: the artist, crew, film, wall, city or event it is about, and what makes it worth a look. Lead with the most striking concrete detail.
2. Use only facts from THE STORY above. Name the artist, crew or place from its tags when it has them. Do not invent techniques, colours, sizes, dates, quotes, reactions or names. If THE STORY says little, write a short post rather than a padded one.
3. Never write about the blog, the archive, the post or the act of publishing: no "Urban Art Core published", "the blog covered", "this post", "years ago today", "anniversary" or "throwback". You may name the year once if it helps place the work in time.
4. Write in an active, plain voice, as a curator recommending something good to a friend. Present tense for the work, past tense for events.
5. No questions to the reader, no emoji, at most one exclamation mark. Never open with "Check out", "Dive into", "Discover", "Did you know" or "Throwback".
6. Keep the text under 240 characters. Bluesky allows 300 characters including the hashtags, which are appended after your text.
7. Do not put the URL in the text. Bluesky shows the link as a card under the post.
8. Do not write hashtags, in the text or anywhere else. Three hashtags made from the story's tags are added after your text.

OUTPUT: a JSON object with "text" (the post text, without hashtags).
